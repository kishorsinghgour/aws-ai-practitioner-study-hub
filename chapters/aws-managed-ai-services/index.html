Key points for quick revision

  • Begin with the input and required output, then choose the most managed service that satisfies the constraints.
  • Rekognition handles visual content; Textract handles document text and structure.
  • Comprehend analyzes text; Transcribe converts speech to text; Polly converts text to speech; Translate changes language.
  • Lex builds bounded conversational flows with intents and slots.
  • Personalize ranks items or actions from interaction data.
  • Bedrock provides managed foundation models for generative applications.
  • SageMaker AI supports custom model building, training, and hosting; JumpStart accelerates work with pre-trained models.
  • Amazon Quick in the exam guide corresponds to the product currently documented as AWS Quick.
  • Managed does not mean risk-free. Evaluate quality, permissions, privacy, region, latency, cost, and human-review needs.
